University of Graz

  • Microeconometrics (Master; co-taught; language: English). Formal introduction to econometrics with emphasis on assumptions and identification. Topics included cross-sectional microeconometric methods for binary, ordered, and multinomial outcomes, as well as censored and truncated models such as Tobit and Heckit models. Based on Cameron and Trivedi (2005), Microeconometrics: Methods and Applications.
  • Econometrics 1 & 2 (Bachelor; independently taught; language: German). Introduction to econometrics through lectures and applied sessions, covering core empirical methods and their practical implementation. Based on Wooldridge, Introductory Econometrics: A Modern Approach.
  • International Economics (Bachelor; independently taught; language: German). Introduction to international trade theory. Based on Krugman, Obstfeld, and Melitz, International Economics: Theory and Policy.
  • Economic Policy (Bachelor; co-taught; language: German). Discussion of economic policy interventions from different models of economic thought, combining abstract policy arguments with concrete and current examples from news articles and ongoing public debates.
